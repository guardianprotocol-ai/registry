# Contributors

Everyone who has contributed to Guardian Protocol, in alphabetical order by last name.

**You do not have to add yourself.** Two of the three things here are already known: your
name comes from your own `Signed-off-by` line, and your GitHub handle is the account that
opened the pull request. Whoever merges fills those in, because asking a contributor to file
paperwork for facts the repository already holds is friction for nothing.

The published credit list is generated by `scripts/build_contributors.py` from the
`Signed-off-by` lines in the git history, joined with this table and with the `credits` in
pattern files. **Signing off is what credits you.** This table only decides how you are
shown.

**So this file is the opt-out as much as the opt-in.** Correct your row, change how your name
reads, name an employer, or ask for your row to be removed, and it will be. Nothing here is
inferred about where you work.

- **GitHub** is optional. Leave it out and your name appears without a link.
- **Organization** is how you opt your employer in. Leave it blank, or write `Individual`,
  and no organization is listed for you. Nothing reads your commit email domain.

The generated list is ordered by the date of each person's first contribution, so it reads
as a history rather than a ranking. Organizations are labelled "Contributing organizations",
never "partners": being listed says someone contributed, not that anyone endorses anything.
It is a different list from the "Built on Guardian Protocol" directory, which stays
alphabetical under `GOVERNANCE.md`.
